One authorization. Embedded Signup v4 collects every asset in a single dialog, then this server subscribes your app to all of them so webhooks start flowing immediately.

Build the login configuration

    

      App Dashboard → Facebook Login for Business → Configurations → Create configuration.
      Pick Embedded Signup as the login variation, then tick every product below — that is what makes one dialog cover all four channels.
    

    


    
Add these permissions too

    
The products auto-select what they need for ads. These extras are what make DM automation and comment replies work.

    


    
Client OAuth settings

    
Facebook Login for Business → Settings. All four toggles must be Yes, and the domain below must be listed under Allowed Domains or the SDK will refuse to return a code.

    


    
Before you start

    
    
      
  • •
    Your app must be a Business type app.
    • 
      
  • •
    Subscribe to the account_update webhook field on whatsapp_business_account — Meta fires it the moment a customer completes the flow, and it carries their business info.
    • 
      
  • •
    In development you can only onboard people with a role on the app (App Roles → Roles). Serving businesses you do not own needs Advanced Access through App Review.
    • 
      
  • •
    Embedded Signup v2 is deprecated on 15 October 2026. Creating a configuration with products selected puts you on v4 automatically.
